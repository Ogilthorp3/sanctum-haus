## What it is

A multi-agent AI council — eight specialized agents that handle different domains (security, health, finance, family communications, infrastructure, satellite outposts, daily wisdom) — orchestrated through a single voice agent on your phone.

It runs entirely on your hardware. A used Mac Mini ($600 on eBay) is enough. No GPU server farm. No OpenAI bill.

When the WiFi goes down, your council still works.
When the cloud has an outage, your screen-time enforcement still works.
When OpenAI changes their terms, your council still belongs to you.

---

## Why now

The last two years have established a clear pattern: **every centralized AI service eventually betrays its users**. Pricing changes. Data-retention reversals. Censorship that varies by country. Models that quietly get worse to save inference cost. Subscriptions that disappear when the company pivots.

The prepper-tech parent already saw this movie with social media, with cloud storage, with smart-home platforms that bricked themselves. They know how it ends.

Sanctum is the *thing they were going to have to build themselves anyway* — but built once, for everyone.

## FAQ (short)

**Does it require an internet connection?** No, except for the first model download. Inference is local.

**Does it require an M-series Mac?** Yes for now. Linux and Windows support is on the roadmap; we're focused on shipping v1 first.

**What's the minimum spec?** Mac Mini M4 with 16 GB unified memory ($599). 32 GB is more comfortable. 64 GB unlocks the full 35B council model.

**Will it really still work when the cloud goes down?** Yes — tested, on purpose, more than once. There's a video.

**Why "Sanctum"?** Because "smart home" is what marketers call it and "haus" is what it actually is.
