# Demo Video Draft v1 — 75 seconds

Format: screen recording + voiceover, no presenter on camera. Shoot at native Mac resolution, scaled in post. Music: ambient/synth, low. Tone: calm-confident, not breathless. Inspired by: Apple Park videos, Rick Rubin podcast intros, Tailscale's launch reel.

---

## SCRIPT

**[0:00–0:05] COLD OPEN**
Black screen. Soft synth pad fades in.

White text, San Francisco font:
> The first private AI hub for your family.

Cut.

**[0:05–0:15] Hero shot**
Mac Mini on a bookshelf, reading lamp on, faint teal LED. Camera slowly pushes in.

Voiceover (calm):
> Sanctum is a council of AI agents that runs on this. One Mac Mini.
> Eight specialists, one orchestrator, your house, your data, your hardware.

**[0:15–0:30] Council in action**
Phone screen recording. iMessage-style interface (Holocron). User taps mic button.

User (voice): *"Yoda, my HRV looked off this morning, can you check what's going on?"*

Screen response (Yoda voice, ElevenLabs, slightly inverted Yoda syntax):
> *"Drop in HRV by twelve points, since Tuesday I see. Cilghal, the data check, hand off to her I will. One moment."*

Quick cut to council activity log on a desktop screen. Yoda routes to Cilghal. Cilghal pulls Apple Watch data. Three lines of structured response come back. The user gets a clean text reply on their phone.

Voiceover:
> Eight agents, each with their domain. Yoda routes. Cilghal handles biometric data. Windu watches the network. Each one trained to stay in their lane.

**[0:30–0:45] The unplug moment**
Cut to the Mac Mini bookshelf again. Camera pushes to the WiFi router.
A hand reaches in. Pulls the WiFi router's power cord.
Screen on the Mac shows: `WiFi: not connected`.

Voiceover:
> Now the cloud is gone.

User raises phone, taps mic again:
> *"Yoda, what's the time and what's on the family calendar today?"*

Yoda responds (full audio, no glitch):
> *"Three forty-two it is. Soccer practice at five, Albert has. Andreanne's mother for dinner, hmm."*

The phone responds normally.

Voiceover:
> The council didn't notice.

**[0:45–0:60] Privacy + family**
Cut to a screen showing screen-time enforcement: a kid's iPad, the timer ticking down to zero, then locking with a soft chime.

Voiceover:
> Screen time that doesn't depend on Apple's servers.
> Family AI that doesn't train its next model on your kids.
> Conversations that never leave the house unless you put them there.

**[0:60–0:70] What it costs**
Cut to clean type:
> Sanctum Core. Free. Open source. Forever.
> Sanctum Family Pass. One hundred twenty-nine dollars. Lifetime.
> No subscription.

**[0:70–0:75] Close**
Sanctum logo. Tagline:
> **Your house, wittily managed.**
> sanctum.run

Music fades.

## SHOT LIST (production checklist)

1. **Hero Mac Mini** — wide-angle, soft natural light from window, evening warm tone. 5 sec, slow push-in.
2. **iPhone Holocron screen** — capture with QuickTime + iPhone Mirroring. Real council interaction. 10 sec.
3. **Desktop council activity log** — screen recording of the Holocron dashboard showing Yoda → Cilghal routing in real time.
4. **WiFi unplug close-up** — camera on tripod, hand reaches in. 5 sec.
5. **Post-unplug iPhone** — same Holocron interface, shows offline indicator, demonstrates response works anyway.
6. **Kid's iPad screen-time lock** — tasteful, no kid in shot. 5 sec.
7. **Clean type cards** — final pricing + tagline.

## VOICEOVER NOTES

- Recommend a non-Bert voice for the VO (the prepper-parent audience trusts the third-party voice over the founder's). Suggested: a calm, slightly textured voice — not a polished announcer. Think NPR-style.
- ElevenLabs Yoda voice for the in-product audio.
- Keep music sparse. Music should not lead the emotion; the mic-tap → voice-response cadence does.

## RUNTIME

Aim: 70-75 sec for HN/Twitter. Don't go over 90.
