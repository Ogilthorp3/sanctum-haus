# Visual Language — Sanctum Launch

Reuses the existing pencil-sketch aesthetic from sanctum-docs (the "apple-deadpool-holocron-valve" voice has a matching visual: dark-mode pencil sketches with localized teal accent lighting, no text, no faces). Brings it into the consumer-marketing context without losing the voice.

## Color palette

- **Background:** near-black `#0B0E11` (matches sanctum-docs dark theme)
- **Primary text:** soft white `#E8EAEC`
- **Accent / CTA:** existing Sanctum teal `#34D9C7` — used sparingly, a single accent per scene
- **Warm secondary:** amber `#E2B670` for "lamp glow" / "fireplace" / "haus warmth" moments
- **Type:** San Francisco for body, Newsreader for display headers (matches recent sanctum-docs typography commit)

## Imagery

Three modes, all hand-drawn pencil-sketch with selective color:

1. **The haus mode** — Mac Mini, bookshelves, desk lamp, plant, slipper, mug. Cozy. Faint teal LED on the Mini. Used for "this lives in your house" beats.
2. **The council mode** — eight stylized figures around a circular table, drawn like Tarot cards, no faces (silhouettes), each with a single object that identifies them (Yoda's cane, Mothma's antenna, Windu's lock, etc.). Used for "what is the council" beats.
3. **The threshold mode** — gates, doors, lanterns, mist, keyholes. Used for "boundary / privacy / mTLS / refusal" beats. Inherits from existing sanctum-docs hero illustrations.

## Anti-patterns (do NOT do)

- No stock photos of people in offices
- No "AI brain" cliché imagery (neural networks, glowing nodes, robot hands)
- No hexagonal grids
- No purple gradient (Anthropic-coded, dilutes the brand)
- No emoji in marketing copy (consistent with the no-emoji rule across sanctum-docs)
- No "POWERED BY OPENAI" badges or model logos. Sanctum is neutral on what runs underneath.

## Photographic shots (for the demo video)

Real-world photography only for the Mac Mini hero, the WiFi-unplug moment, and the kid's iPad. All other content is screen-recorded. Keep camera work simple — tripod-fixed, slow push-ins, no handheld.

## Brand voice — copy rules

- **The council is real.** Yoda *is* an agent named Yoda; we don't apologize for the Star Wars reference. The audience is geeky parents — they get it.
- **"Your haus" not "your home"** — matches the doctrine memory.
- **"The cloud" is a noun we use sparingly.** Don't bash it; let the value prop do the work.
- **No exclamation marks** in marketing copy. Sanctum is calm.
- **Numbers are friends.** "50+ tokens/sec on M-series Mac." "Eight agents." "$129 lifetime." Specifics earn trust faster than adjectives.

## Reference shots / mood

- Apple Park product films (the calm, the slow push-in, the warm lamp light)
- Plex Pass announcement page (the way they sell perpetual licenses)
- Tailscale launch reel (the technical-but-warm voiceover)
- Sanctum docs operations chapters (the sketch hero illustrations)
